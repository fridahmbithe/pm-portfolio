About

From intern to Associate Product Owner, in under two years.

I started as a Project Management Intern at Roamtech Solutions and now own delivery for Senti, a WhatsApp-based lending line, and co-own the roadmap for Paykit, Roamtech's payments product — running discovery, requirements, and launch across distributed engineering and business teams.

My work sits where product delivery meets compliance and data: I've taken loan servicing and credit-scoring from fully manual to fully automated, translated CBK regulatory guidance into product requirements for sanctions screening and reporting, and made the harder call when the numbers demanded it — recommending against building a loan product after field research showed weak demand and elevated default risk.

I gravitate toward product work with real consequences attached — where a bad requirement doesn't just mean a delayed release, it means a customer gets wrongly flagged, a regulator asks questions, or a loan goes to someone who can't repay it. That's part of why I stayed close to compliance and credit risk rather than treating them as someone else's problem to hand off.

As a product owner, I use AI tools daily to move faster on documentation, requirements, and prototyping — and I'm comfortable picking up new tools and ambiguity fast.
